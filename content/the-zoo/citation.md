# How to cite Quantum Benchmark Zoo

Thank you for your interest in **Quantum Benchmark Zoo**! If you find our platform useful, please consider citing us to acknowledge our contributors and support the project's continued development.

You can use the following BibTeX entry for this purpose:

```bibtex
@misc{valentin2025quantum,
  title={Quantum Benchmark Zoo},
  author={Valentin, Gilbert and Antoine, Croisille and Luca, Phab and Julien, Rodriguez and Daniel, Vert and Gabriella, Bettonte and Rasha, Friji and Olivier, Rousselle and Alejandro, Montanez-Barrera and Ward, Van Der Schoot and others},
  url={https://quantumbenchmarkzoo.org},
  year={2025}
}
```
